Chapter Two

A Simple Observation

HydraSmart began when we noticed something obvious yet overlooked: people already have bottles they love. They don't need another gadget—they need intelligence added to what they already use.

During pregnancy and postpartum, dehydration can quickly lead to fatigue, complications, and unnecessary medical intervention. Yet guidance remains generic and reactive. We built HydraSmart to remove guesswork from hydration by quietly measuring real intake and translating it into actionable, personalized targets.
